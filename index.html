Services

Practical, protocol-level help for researchers and companies working with fluorescence imaging and cleared tissue.

Fluorescence microscopy advice

Microscope and system selection, imaging strategy, acquisition settings and analysis planning, matched to your samples and budget.

Tissue clearing

Choosing between clearing methods, adapting and optimising protocols, and troubleshooting problems such as shrinkage, opacity or signal loss.

Fluorescent labelling

Dye and antibody selection, multiplexing panels, whole-mount immunolabelling and improving photostability in cleared samples.

3D printing & design

Custom lab hardware, sample holders, adapters and low-cost equipment, designed and made to be printable almost anywhere.

Protocols & documentation

Reviewing the literature to extract the practical details, then turning them into clear, step-by-step protocols and written guidance your team can follow.

Support for industry

Technical support for companies supplying microscopy and sample preparation products, including application notes and customer-facing guidance.

About

I'm Dr Peter Gordon, an imaging scientist with more than a decade of experience across cancer immunology and neuroscience. I develop tissue clearing, labelling and imaging methods for a wide range of samples and species, working across light microscopy from confocal and super-resolution to lightsheet, serial section two-photon and intravital imaging, paired with full-organ immunolabelling and multiplexed RNA labelling.

Much of my work means building approaches that don't exist yet, supported by my own CAD design, 3D printing and automation. I also lead multidisciplinary projects, train and supervise researchers, and collaborate with academic groups and supplier companies internationally. I'm at my best taking on an unfamiliar problem, finding a practical route through it, and turning complex methods into reliable tools that other researchers can use.
